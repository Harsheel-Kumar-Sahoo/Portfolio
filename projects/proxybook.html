About the project

Who covered which class for whom?

A proxy is when one friend covers a class for another, for example by marking attendance. In our college batch, proxies were being tracked from memory, which led to arguments about who owed what. I built ProxyBook to fix that: a shared ledger where every proxy is logged, confirmed by the other person, and settled when you are square.

Key features

  • Running balance for every friend: three things change a balance: you did a proxy, they did a proxy, or you settle up. Settling brings the balance to zero and history stays.
  • Synced friends: add a friend by their ProxyBook username and you confirm each other’s proxies. A proxy counts once it is accepted, so both ledgers always match.
  • Friends without a username: add just a name, and optionally a phone number, for people who have not joined yet.
  • Log in a few taps: choose the subject, set 1 to 20 classes, pick the date and add a note.
  • Requests inbox: accept, decline or cancel requests, with a bell badge showing how many are waiting.
  • Home screen with tally marks: the total owed to you is shown as tally marks, with a yellow slash closing each group of five.
  • Ledger with search: see every friend’s balance and their last 10 entries.
  • WhatsApp nudges: send a polite reminder to a friend who owes you. Without a saved number, the message is copied instead.
  • Activity and undo: nothing is ever erased. Undo adds a matching entry that cancels the mistake, so the numbers always add up.
  • Accounts and settings: unique usernames, name and password changes, light and dark mode, and account deletion.
  • Auto-refresh: new requests and tally marks appear on their own.

How it works

An example

You did 2 proxies for NehaBalance +2. She owes you 2.
Neha did 1 proxy for youBalance +1. She now owes you 1.
Neha did 3 more for youYou owe 2. The balance flips.
You tap Settle upSettled. Back to zero, history stays.
