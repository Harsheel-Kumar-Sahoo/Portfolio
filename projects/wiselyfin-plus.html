About the project

Learn. Play. Grow financially.

WiselyFin Plus is the sequel to my earlier project, WiselyFin. It is a financial-literacy app made for India, built around short lessons, daily challenges and simple money tools, so that learning about money feels like a habit and not a chore.

Key features

  • Short lessons in 5 languages: a language switcher sits at the top of every screen.
  • Daily challenges: a quick question each day that earns FinCoins and XP.
  • Levels, XP and day streaks: progress you can see, to keep you learning a little every day.
  • Calculators and a budget tracker: practical money tools in one place.
  • Practice investing with virtual money: try out ideas with no real money at risk.
  • Accounts with email verification, a wallet and a profile, plus a light and dark theme.
  • Mobile-first navigation: Home, Learn, Tools, Wallet and Profile.
