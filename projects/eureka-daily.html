About the project

What’s special about today in science?

Eureka Daily shows what is special about today in the world of science. For every date there are 3 to 5 discoveries, births and milestones, and each one is explained in about 100 words, so you can learn something new in a minute.

Key features

  • Fresh every day: every date has its own set of events, and a date strip lets you move between days.
  • Around the world: science events from across the globe that happened on that date.
  • India in science: a section for Indian contributions to science.
  • Tagged by field: each entry shows its field (for example Space or Physics) and its year.
  • Short explanations: about 100 words each, covering discoveries, births and milestones.
  • Light and dark mode.
